# Dashboard Guide

## Overview

The Pharmaceutical Prescription Analytics Dashboard provides an interactive view of prescription activity, healthcare spending, prescriber performance, and opioid/antibiotic prescribing trends across the United States.

The dashboard is organized into three analytical pages, each designed to answer a different business question—from executive-level healthcare spending trends to prescriber performance and opioid prescribing analysis.

---

# Page 1 – Executive Overview

## Purpose
Provides a high-level summary of nationwide prescription activity and healthcare expenditure.

### Key Performance Indicators
- Total Claims
- Total Drug Spending
- Total Beneficiaries
- Avg Cost per Claim
- Generic Rate

### Visualizations
- **Drug Spending by State** (Filled Map) — visualizes the geographic distribution of prescription drug spending across U.S. states while allowing users to explore claims and beneficiary metrics through interactive tooltips
- **Top 10 Medical Specialties by Total Drug Spending** (Clustered Bar Chart) — Highlights which medical specialties contribute most to overall prescription drug spending.

---

# Page 2 – Prescriber Performance

## Purpose
Evaluates prescribing behavior at the individual provider level.

### Key Performance Indicators
- Total Prescribers
- Avg Claims per Prescriber
- Avg Spending per Prescriber

### Visualizations
- **Top 10 Prescribers by Total Claims** (Clustered Bar Chart) — identifies the highest-volume prescribers based on total prescription claims
- **Top 10 Prescribers by Total Drug Spending** (Clustered Bar Chart) — highlights prescribers responsible for the highest overall drug expenditure
- **Prescriber Performance Details** (Table) — detailed breakdown by National Provider Identifier (NPI), including specialty, state, total claims, drug spending, average cost per claim, and generic prescribing rate

---

# Page 3 – Opioid & Antibiotic Analysis

## Purpose
Analyzes prescription trends for opioid and antibiotic medications to support healthcare monitoring and policy decisions.

### Key Performance Indicators
- Total Opioid Spending
- Total Antibiotic Spending
- Total Opioid Claims
- Total Antibiotic Claims

### Visualizations
- **Top 10 States by Opioid Claims** (Bar Chart)
- **Top 10 Specialties by Opioid Spending** (Bar Chart)
